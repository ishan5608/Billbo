Billbo: no more bag of bills

A working prototype of the idea. Three screens show the three people in the story.

1. Owner chat

The shop owner's WhatsApp-style chat with Billbo. Send sample bill photos, voice notes and notices, and watch the agents answer.

2. Accountant dashboard

The accountant sees every client sorted and checked, a review queue, a filing-ready pack, and what Billbo costs him.

3. Mock GST portal

A stand-in for the government portal. Suppliers report invoices here, and a human files. Billbo never does.

Try this 2-minute demo

  1. Open Owner chat as Ramesh (Billbo Plus). Send the bill Mahadev Plastics, MP-2231. Billbo saves it and warns that the credit is at risk. Reply yes to get a supplier reminder.
  2. Send the blurry bill. Billbo asks before guessing. Reply yes.
  3. Send the voice note order going to Pune, then type a vehicle number such as KA19AB1234.
  4. Open the Mock GST portal and click Supplier reports this invoice. Go back to the chat: Billbo says the credit is now safe.
  5. Open the Accountant dashboard: the month is sorted, with a review queue and a downloadable pack.
  6. Switch the chat to Fathima (Free plan) and send the same bill to see how the paid features are held back.

What is simulated: bill photos and voice notes are samples standing in for a vision and speech model, the chat stands in for WhatsApp, and the portal is a mock. What is real: the six agents, the routing, the rule engine, the credit matching, the plan limits and the audit trail.
